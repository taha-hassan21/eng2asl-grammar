Core ASL Grammar Rules (Time - Topic - Comment)

1. TIME FIRST

Time indicators (YESTERDAY, TOMORROW, NOW) always go at the very beginning of the sentence to set the tense.

2. TOPIC + COMMENT

Establish the main subject/object first (MOVIE), then sign the action or description (I WATCH).

3. DROP LINKING WORDS

Articles (a, an, the) and linking verbs (is, am, are, was, were) are omitted in ASL Gloss.

4. QUESTIONS AT END

WH-words (WHO, WHAT, WHERE, WHY) move to the very end with furrowed eyebrows.
