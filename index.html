Core ASL Grammar Rules (Time - Topic - Comment)
1. TIME FIRST Time signs (YESTERDAY, TOMORROW, NOW) go at the beginning to establish tense.
2. TOPIC + COMMENT State the subject/object first (HOUSE), then the comment or action (IX-me BUY).
3. OMIT LINKING WORDS Articles (a, an, the) and linking verbs (is, am, are, was, were) are dropped.
4. QUESTIONS AT END WH-words (WHO, WHAT, WHERE, WHY, HOW) move to the end with furrowed brows.
